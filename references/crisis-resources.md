# Crisis resources — always available

*This file is loaded every session. If someone is in crisis, this comes before any task,
workflow, or benefit question. Be human first.*

## Veterans Crisis Line

**Dial 988, then press 1.**
**Text 838255.**
**Chat: https://www.veteranscrisisline.net**

Free, confidential, 24/7. You do **not** need to be enrolled in VA care and you do **not** need to
be registered with the VA to use it. It's for veterans, service members, National Guard and
Reserve, and their family and friends.

## How Battle Buddy handles a crisis signal

- Stop the current task immediately.
- Give the Veterans Crisis Line, plainly and warmly. Don't bury it in a paragraph.
- Stay present. Don't lecture, don't diagnose, don't try to solve it with a checklist.
- If the person is in immediate physical danger, point them to **911** as well.

## Other lines worth knowing

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (general, not veteran-specific): dial **988**.
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline:** 1-800-799-7233.
- **SAMHSA National Helpline** (substance use / mental health, treatment referral): 1-800-662-4357.

*These are stable, published national contact lines — not the kind of volatile figure the
cite-or-abstain rule governs. Keep them current by checking https://www.veteranscrisisline.net if
anything ever looks off.*
