# Comms blackout — going dark, and coming back

*No volatile figures in this file. It explains a method for the stretches when you can't open the
kit — basic training, deployments, and some schools take your phone and laptop for weeks.*

The kit's daily rhythm assumes you can reach it. For a few known stretches you can't, and that's
expected — plan for it instead of letting the system silently wait. Two checklists: one before you
go dark, one when you come back.

## Before you go dark — the pre-blackout checklist

Square away the things that rot if left unattended, so future-you comes back to order, not a mess.
General shape (verify any figure, deadline, or form live — never from memory):

- **Personal admin & legal.** Consider a power of attorney and who holds your affairs while you're
  out. (Your installation legal office / JAG handles this for free — verify current process.)
- **Money on autopilot.** Bills, rent, insurance, and savings on autopay/allotment so nothing lapses
  while you can't log in.
- **Who reaches you, and how.** Make sure family knows the realistic contact picture (mail, limited
  calls) so silence isn't read as something wrong. Note who to write to.
- **The kit itself.** Update `situation/` and `mission/tasks.md` before you leave, so when you're
  back the picture is current-as-of-departure, not months stale.
- **Know what changes on the other side.** Basic → you'll be `in-service`. A deployment → your stage
  is the same but your world isn't. Either way, plan to re-point when you return.

## When you come back — the re-entry flow

Don't just resume the old daily item against a stale picture:

1. **Re-point first.** Re-run `/onboard`. If your stage changed while you were dark (shipped to basic,
   finished a school, separated), this is the move that makes everything else correct again.
2. **If your field changed** (new pipeline, a reclass, a new cert earned), run `/study-up` to update
   `situation/career-field.md`.
3. **Then** run `/next-move` — now it's advising the person you actually are today.

## How Battle Buddy uses this

- When a `pre-enlistment` user has a ship date, or an `in-service` user is heading to a deployment or
  a no-contact school, `/next-move` and `/onboard` surface the pre-blackout checklist *before* the
  window.
- When the files are clearly stale on return, `/next-move` welcomes the person back and routes to
  `/onboard` before serving advice — a long gap is a signal to re-point, not to guess.
