# The enlistment path — from curious to shipped

> **Verified:** 2026-07-21 · **Contains no volatile figures.** Score cutoffs, bonuses, and ship
> timelines change and vary by branch and job — pull those live and cite them. This file is the
> map and the questions to ask.

For a pre-enlistment user (who may be 17): present the real tradeoffs, apply no pressure, and
never contradict "talk to your parents or a trusted adult." The goal is an informed decision, not
a signature.

## The steps, in order

1. **Talk to recruiters — plural.** A recruiter works for one branch. Talk to more than one
   before deciding. They are a resource, not an enemy — but their job is to fill slots, so you
   verify what you're told.
2. **ASVAB.** The aptitude test. Your **AFQT** score (derived from four ASVAB sections) sets basic
   eligibility; **line scores** determine which jobs you qualify for. Study — it directly widens
   your options.
3. **MEPS** (Military Entrance Processing Station) — medical exam, background, and where you may
   review a contract. Don't sign anything you don't understand.
4. **The contract.** This is the whole game. See "what's negotiable" below.
5. **DEP** (Delayed Entry Program) — you've committed but haven't shipped. Useful time: get in
   shape, lock details, ask questions. You can still raise concerns during DEP.
6. **Ship to basic.** Stage becomes `in-service` — re-run `/onboard`.

## What's actually negotiable (before you sign)

This is where a good decision is made or lost. Everything here should be **in writing in the
contract**, not a verbal promise:

- **Guaranteed job vs. open contract.** A guaranteed job locks your career field before you ship.
  An "open" contract lets the needs of the service assign you. If you care what you'll do, get it
  guaranteed and in writing.
- **Enlistment bonus** — for which jobs, how much, paid when, with what strings. Verify the
  current figures against the official source, not the pitch.
- **Ship date** — affects bonuses, seasons, and your prep time.
- **Rank at entry** — prior JROTC, college credits, certain programs can start you higher.
- **Length of contract** and any post-service reserve obligation.

## Questions to bring to a recruiter

- What jobs do my line scores actually qualify me for?
- Is the job I want *guaranteed in writing*, or am I signing open?
- What exactly does the bonus require, and when is it paid?
- What does the full obligation look like — active plus reserve?
- What happens if I don't qualify for the job I wanted at MEPS?

## Documented red flags

- "Sign now, we'll add the job later." Get it in the contract or treat it as not promised.
- Pressure to decide today.
- Any verbal promise that isn't written down.
- Being told not to talk to family or another branch.
