# Organizations worth knowing — the free help that already exists

> **Verified:** 2026-07-21 · **Contains no volatile figures.** A directory of who does what and
> their official site. Most people under-use this list; `/next-move` and `/benefits-check` will
> point you at the right one.

## File a disability claim — for FREE

**Never pay a "claim shark."** Accredited Veterans Service Officers file claims at no cost and are
trained to do it right. Use one.

- **Your county / state Veterans Service Officer** — often the best first call; search
  "[your county] veterans service officer."
- **DAV (Disabled American Veterans):** https://www.dav.org
- **VFW (Veterans of Foreign Wars):** https://www.vfw.org/assistance/va-claims-separation
- **The American Legion:** https://www.legion.org/veteransbenefits
- **VA's accredited-representative search:** https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/

## Find a job / a mentor

- **Hiring Our Heroes** (US Chamber) — hiring events, fellowships: https://www.hiringourheroes.org
- **FourBlock** — career-readiness program: https://www.fourblock.org
- **American Corporate Partners (ACP)** — free 1:1 year-long mentorship:
  https://www.acp-usa.org
- **Veterati** — free on-demand mentorship calls: https://www.veterati.com
- **O2O / Onward to Opportunity (Syracuse IVMF)** — free career training:
  https://ivmf.syracuse.edu

## Transition & entrepreneurship

- **DoD SkillBridge** — civilian internship in your final stretch: https://skillbridge.osd.mil
- **SBA — Boots to Business** (veteran entrepreneurship): https://www.sba.gov/boots-to-business
- **VBOC (Veterans Business Outreach Centers):** https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance

## Wellness & peer connection

- **Team RWB** (Red, White & Blue) — fitness + community: https://www.teamrwb.org
- **Team Rubicon** — disaster response, purpose after service: https://www.teamrubiconusa.org
- **Veterans Crisis Line** — see `crisis-resources.md`.

## How Battle Buddy uses this

When a skill hits something better handled by a human or an org — a claim, a mentor match, a
training slot — it names the specific free option here and drafts the outreach for the person's
review. It doesn't pretend to be the VSO; it gets them to one.
